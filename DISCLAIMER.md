# Disclaimer — Menstrual Cycle Companion

## Purpose and usage
This integration/card is for orientation, self-tracking, and pattern recognition only.
It is not a medical device and not a substitute for medical diagnosis, advice, or treatment.

## Health and medical limits
Calculations are based on simplified models and historical input.
If you experience pain, unusual bleeding, missed periods, strong cycle irregularities, or other concerning symptoms, seek medical advice.

## Contraception and conception planning
This view is not suitable for reliable contraception.
This view is also not suitable as a sole basis for conception planning decisions.
For reliable decisions, use validated methods and professional guidance.

## Consent, mutual agreement, responsible use
Use, sharing, and interpretation of data should happen only with voluntary, informed, and mutual consent.
No pressure, coercion, surveillance, or control of other persons.
Decisions about sexuality, contraception, and family planning must remain respectful, autonomous, and consensual.

## Privacy and data protection
Cycle data is highly sensitive health data.
Collect only what is necessary, restrict access, protect backups, and add safeguards for cloud/remote access.
Responsibility for storage, access control, and data sharing remains with the operating user/instance.

For people who may face third-party access risks, it is possible to trigger a per-sensor `erase_all_history` service call. This removes the current stored history for that sensor.

Data may also exist in other systems such as databases, backups, or external dashboards. Those copies must be assessed and deleted separately by the operator when appropriate.

Data recovery after deletion cannot be promised or guaranteed.
